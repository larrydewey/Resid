tsm_report (configfs /sys/kernel/config/tsm/report/$name/)
  written     inblob (at most TSM_REPORT_INBLOB_MAX, 64), privlevel (0..3, at
              least privlevel_floor), service_provider ("svsm"),
              service_guid, service_manifest_version
  read        outblob, auxblob, manifestblob, provider, generation,
              privlevel_floor
  generation  each *_store (and inblob's write) calls
              try_advance_write_generation once per write(2):
              service_guid's before guid_parse (a bad GUID still counts),
              privlevel's and service_manifest_version's after parsing,
              service_provider's first
  providers   the drivers' KBUILD_MODNAME, `-` made `_`:
              sev_guest, tdx_guest, arm_cca_guest
  inblob      sev_guest exactly 64 (SNP_REPORT_USER_DATA_SIZE); tdx_guest
              exactly 64 (TDX_REPORTDATA_LEN); arm_cca_guest 32 to 64
  visible     sev_guest: privlevel, privlevel_floor, auxblob; under an SVSM
              also service_provider, service_guid, service_manifest_version,
              manifestblob. tdx_guest: inblob, outblob, provider, generation
              only. arm_cca_guest: no visibility hooks, so every attribute
              shows; privlevel and service_provider are stored and ignored.
  maxima      TDX quote GET_QUOTE_BUF_SIZE (128 KiB) less its header; SNP
              report within 4 KiB; SNP certificates SEV_FW_BLOB_MAX_SIZE
              (16 KiB); SVSM manifest 4 KiB, more on an SVSM-requested retry;
              any blob at most TSM_REPORT_OUTBLOB_MAX (16 MiB)

pci_tsm (sysfs)
  /sys/class/tsm/tsmN                       tsm-core.c, "tsm%d" from an IDA
  /sys/bus/pci/devices/.../tsm/connect      RW, "tsmN\n" or "\n"
  /sys/bus/pci/devices/.../tsm/disconnect   WO
  /sys/bus/pci/devices/.../tsm/dsm          RO, the DSM's PCI name
  /sys/bus/pci/devices/.../tsm/bound        RO, "tsmN\n" or "\n"
  /sys/bus/pci/devices/.../authenticated    RO, the connect state
  /sys/devices/pciDDDD:BB/available_secure_streams, streamH.R.E
  No measurement, certificate or interface-report attribute exists.
